About Ydil Woods

Uplifting trance, melody first.

I write euphoric, emotional trance built around simple, memorable melodies — the kind you're still humming two days later. No overcrowded breaks, no filler: every layer is there to lift the emotion.

My tracks live where the energy peaks: festival mainstages, sunrise sets and late-night drives. Recent releases include Chasing Starlight, Pure Energy and Infinite Embrace, with new music on the way every few weeks.

For fans of Ben Gold, Ralphie B and classic uplifting energy.

DJs & labels: supporting my music in your set or show? Reach out for WAVs and extended mixes — contact via Instagram @ydilwoods.
